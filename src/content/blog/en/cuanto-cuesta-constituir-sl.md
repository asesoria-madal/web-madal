What it costs to set up an SL depends mainly on the route: through CIRCE, with standard articles, notary and Registry fees are set; through the traditional route, with custom articles, they aren’t. Some of the higher figures you’ll see come from adding the share capital, which isn’t an expense, or a law firm’s fees.

## Through CIRCE, with standard articles

This is the most common and cheapest route. Notary and Registry fees depend on the capital:

| Item | Up to €3,100 of capital | Over €3,100 of capital |
|---|---|---|
| Notary | €60 + VAT | €150 + VAT |
| Commercial Registry | €40 + VAT | €100 + VAT |
| Name certificate | A small amount, set by the Central Commercial Registry | Same |
| Accountant | Depends on who handles it | Depends on who handles it |

To file through CIRCE, a partner must top up €121 in the [Virtual Wallet](/en/blog/cartera-virtual-circe). This isn’t an extra expense: the Registry fee is deducted from it and what’s left can be recovered. You do have to pay it up front, though.

## Through the traditional route, with custom articles

This makes sense if you need something standard articles don’t allow, such as a board of directors or specific clauses between partners. Those reduced fees don’t apply here, and the cost depends on each case:

| Item | Rough guide |
|---|---|
| Notary and Registry | Around €200 to €350 in total, based on figures published by accounting firms |
| Drafting the articles and legal advice | Depends on the firm. With a lawyer, some comparisons mention €500 to €2,000 in total |
| Name certificate | A small amount, set by the Central Commercial Registry |

These are commercial figures, not official ones, so treat them as a guide and always ask for a quote. We explain when each route is worthwhile in [CIRCE or the traditional route](/en/blog/montar-sl-circe-o-tradicional).

## What to ask when requesting a quote

Few firms publish their price, which makes comparing hard. Ask whether it includes the name certificate, preparing the documents and the notary appointment, and whether the amounts include VAT.

## What isn’t a cost

You can start with as little as €1, and the **share capital** remains the company’s money. Below €3,000 it comes with two conditions, which we explain in [minimum capital for an SL](/en/blog/capital-minimo-sl).

## What comes after setting it up

The cost of an SL doesn’t end at registration. Also bear in mind:

- **Monthly accounting**, because a company has accounting and tax obligations from day one. Our SL plans start at €100 + VAT a month.
