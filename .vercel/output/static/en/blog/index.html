Frequently asked questions: Transparent answers

FAQs and articles about taxes

Freelancers

How long does freelancer registration take?

One business day, if your paperwork is ready.

Flat-rate or direct estimation regime?

It depends on your activity. We explain it on the first call.

What if I don’t invoice anything in a quarter?

We file a zero return. It’s included in your fee.

Is the annual income tax return included?

No, it’s billed separately, from €40.

Can I opt for the flat-rate (módulos) regime if my activity qualifies?

Yes, if your activity is on the allowed list and you stay under the invoicing limits. If you don’t request it when registering, direct estimation applies by default.

What’s the difference between a balance sheet and a profit and loss statement?

The profit and loss statement shows whether you made or lost money over a period; the balance sheet shows whether your business is solvent at a specific moment.

How often should I check my business’s balance sheet?

Quarterly is a good rhythm — enough time to spot a trend without reacting too late.

Why does the price of an accounting firm vary so much between businesses?

It depends on your tax regime, how many invoices you issue, whether you have employees, and whether you want reporting on top of tax filing.

When does Verifactu actually become mandatory?

1 January 2027 for companies and 1 July 2027 for freelancers, per Royal Decree-Law 15/2025 — not July 2026, which is the outdated date still circulating.

Who does Verifactu affect?

Only people who invoice through billing software (a SIF). If you invoice by hand or on paper, this specific obligation doesn’t apply to you.

Companies (SL)

Do you handle accounting for an SL?

Yes, if it’s a company with simple accounting.

Do you manage payroll?

Basic payroll, yes. For larger teams, we refer you to a specialist.

Can you take over an SL that’s already running?

Yes, we handle the handover with your current accounting firm.

Do you help incorporate an SL?

Not yet. We advise you, but incorporation is handled by a notary.
