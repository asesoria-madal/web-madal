How much does an online accounting firm cost in Spain in 2026

18 August 2026

There’s no fixed market rate for an accounting firm (gestoría) in Spain. The price depends on your specific situation. Here’s what actually moves it, so you know what to ask before signing up with anyone.

What pushes the price up or down

  • Tax regime: filing under the flat-rate regime (módulos) is usually cheaper to manage than direct estimation, since there are fewer expenses to review and justify every quarter.
  • Invoice volume: the more invoices you issue and receive each month, the more bookkeeping work — almost every firm scales price on this basis.
  • Employees: if you have staff, payroll and social security management get added on top of the purely tax-related work.
  • Reporting and extra advisory: filing taxes is the baseline. A balance sheet you can actually read and understand, not just handed to you, is a higher-value service and usually priced separately.

Why a “one price fits all” quote should make you suspicious

If a firm gives you a flat price without asking about your tax regime, your invoice volume, or whether you have staff, that price probably isn’t matched to your real situation — or the service behind it is thinner than it looks.

How we do it

We’d rather you know an approximate price in a minute, through our simulator, by answering exactly those questions (regime, invoices, employees, whether you want reporting). It doesn’t replace an actual conversation, but it gives you an honest number before you commit to anything.
